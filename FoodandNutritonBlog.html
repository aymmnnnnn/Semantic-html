Simple Tips for School

Drink Plenty of Water

Water keeps us fresh and helps us pay attention in class. Keep a water bottle with you.

Eat Rainbow Colors

Try to eat fruits of different colors like red apples, yellow bananas, and green grapes.

Quick Food Note

Fun Tip: Fill half of your lunch box with fresh fruits and veggies every day!
